Feed the Wolf: Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version. Feed the Wolf has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your game progress stays on your own device. If you buy something, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and a payments service called RevenueCat confirms it so the game can unlock what you bought.

Who we are

Feed the Wolf is a puzzle game published by Failure Entertainment ("we", "us"). This policy covers the iPhone and Android apps and the web version at feed-the-wolf.onrender.com.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling tools.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved only on the device you play on: levels cleared, stars, gems, the characters, pets and outfits you own, and your settings. We cannot see it and it is not sent to us. In the apps a second copy is kept in the app's own storage on the same device so an update cannot lose it.

Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in a browser, deletes that progress. Progress is not shared between the web version and the apps, or between devices.

Purchases

The game is free to download. A one-time purchase unlocks the full game, and gem packs are optional. Purchases are available only in the iPhone and Android apps.

WhoWhat they handle
Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play)Your payment. We never receive your card or billing details.
RevenueCatConfirms with Apple or Google that a purchase is genuine, so the game can unlock it and restore it later. It receives a random identifier created for your copy of the app, which products were bought and when, and basic technical details such as app version, device type and country of the store. It does not receive your name or email from us.

Their own policies apply to what they do: Apple, Google, RevenueCat.

The web version

The web version is hosted by Render. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) to run and protect the service. We do not use those logs to identify players. The web version sets no tracking cookies; it uses your browser's storage only to save your game.

Beta testing

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight or a Google Play test, Apple or Google may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us. That feedback can include your email address if you send it through TestFlight. We use it only to fix the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no user-generated content and no links to social media. Purchases require the approval set up on the device's Apple or Google account.

Your choices

  • To erase your game data, delete the app or clear the site's data in your browser.
  • To ask about, or request deletion of, the purchase record held by RevenueCat for your copy of the app, contact us using the details on the support page.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, under their own terms.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page for how to reach us.
