How to play

  • Tap a gold pin to pull it. A floor pin drops whatever is in that cell. A wall pin joins two cells.
  • Lava and water run along the floor and pour down the nearest hole. Where they meet, both vanish in steam.
  • Lava burns dinner and sends anyone it reaches running. Nobody gets hurt, but a wolf who runs goes hungry.
  • The hunter walks to the wolf and nets him once they share a floor. Lava, a falling boulder or a blast sends him out the door.
  • The wolf walks to dinner once they share a floor. Neither he nor the hunter can cross a hole; they fall in.
  • Boulders only fall straight down. They smash cracked wooden floors, flatten dinner and spook the wolf if they land on them.
  • TNT goes off when lava or a boulder hits it and blows out the cracked floor under it. Water soaks it for good.
  • Ice is frozen water that stays put. It cancels lava that touches it.
  • Seesaw: drop a boulder on one end and whatever sits on the other flies through the gap into the next cell.
  • Spring: anything that lands on it bounces through the gap into the next cell, boulders included.
  • Portals (from level 21): whatever drops into the glowing ring on a floor comes out of the matching ring in a ceiling. That includes lava, water, boulders, dinner and the wolf.
  • Ghosts (from level 26) drift to the wolf and scare him off once they share a floor. Lava and boulders pass through them. Water gets rid of them.
  • Gears (Clockwork Citadel) turn when a boulder lands on them and open the gate they are wired to. Numbered gears must be turned in order: turn one early and it strips its teeth for good.
  • Blooms (Moonlit Conservatory) open their gate when water reaches them, and drink a measure of that water doing it. Lava reaching a bloom that has not opened withers it for good.
  • Beacons (Ghostlight Theatre) light when lava reaches them. A lit beacon opens its gate and drives every ghost off that floor. Water reaching an unlit beacon puts it out for good.
  • Daily puzzle: a different castle each day, worth 5 gems the first time you solve it.
  • Caged wolves turn up in hard mode. Walk the wolf to the cage, or break it open with a boulder or a blast. Everyone has to be out before dinner counts, and lava must never reach a cage.
  • Undo takes back your last pull. Hint costs a gem and lights up a pin that still leads to a win.
  • Stars: clear a level with no failed tries for three. Stars are gems, and gems buy all outfits and pets in the Store. Hard mode rebuilds the level you are on.
