Contact us. Email nlubrick@gmail.com for game support, billing questions, bug reports, or privacy requests. Beta testers can also use TestFlight's Share Beta Feedback feature.

When you write to us

These details let us find the problem quickly:

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 16 or Pixel 9) and its software version.
  • The level number shown at the top of the screen.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

Common questions

What is free and what costs money?

The download, levels 1 to 5 and the first mini-game are free. A one-time purchase of $2.99 unlocks all 110 levels, Hard Mode and the daily puzzle, and comes with a free pet and accessory. Gem packs are optional: every character, pet and outfit sold for gems can also be earned by playing. Prices may vary by country.

I bought the full game but it is locked

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with, and that you are online.

I got a new phone. Do I keep my purchases and progress?

Purchases come back with Restore purchases. Progress (levels, stars and gems earned by playing) is saved only on the device, so it does not move to a new phone unless your phone's own backup restores the app's data.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple or Google Play.

I am stuck on a level

Tap Hint to light up a pin that still leads to a win (it costs one gem), or Undo to take back your last pull. Mini-games can always be skipped, and they ease off if you lose three times in a row.

There is no sound

Check your phone's silent switch and volume, then tap the sound button in the game. On iPhone, sound starts after your first tap.

I am testing a beta and the checkout says TEST

That is a practice checkout. No payment is taken and nothing is charged.
